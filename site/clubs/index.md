# Premier League club scorers and streaks

Choose a club in the [interactive directory](https://vardywatch.com/clubs/) for its current-season scorers and active in-season runs. Club links use a query such as https://vardywatch.com/clubs/?club=arsenal.

Every club with a completed or scheduled fixture in the current season is included. Every credited goal stays with the club represented in that fixture, including goals by departed players. Penalties count; own goals do not. Tied goal totals share a rank and skip subsequent places (1, 1, 3), with alphabetical ties.

Active streaks require at least two consecutive completed club fixtures with a credited goal within the season, through the club's latest completed fixture. A fixture without a credited goal ends the run, even if the player did not appear. Active does not establish current squad membership. Select a player for all available club fixtures for that season, last goal and next game.

Statistics load with JavaScript from [published dashboard data](https://vardywatch.com/data/current-season.json); this overview does not embed live statistics. The data's generatedAt timestamp is calculation time, not proof of upstream source freshness.
