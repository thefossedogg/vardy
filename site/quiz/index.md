# Premier League football quiz

Play the latest [published quiz](https://vardywatch.com/quiz/) or browse the [quiz archive](https://vardywatch.com/quiz/archive.md).

Each quiz has 5-10 multiple-choice questions with four options, one question at a time, immediate factual feedback and a final score. JavaScript is required to play. There is no timer, account or leaderboard. Completed attempts and best/latest scores are stored only in this browser, separated by quiz revision; the archive offers a clear-history control. Storage is not cross-device sync.

Questions use a five-day window of verified completed Premier League fixtures: the selected UK date and its previous four calendar days. The exact coverage dates appear at the bottom of each published quiz. Credited goals include penalties and exclude own goals. Player alternatives are verified scorers for the relevant clubs within the same window; score alternatives are actual recorded scorelines within that window, not predictions. A non-scorer is not proof of non-appearance.

Code establishes facts and answer options; AI selects questions and approved wording. An editor reviews the exact revision before publication. Unsupported questions are omitted; fewer than five supported questions prevents publication. Opening-scorer and gameweek claims require verified source evidence and are not inferred from unordered goals or calendar dates. Published revisions stay playable; corrections require a newly reviewed revision.
