# Premier League football quiz

Play the latest [published quiz](https://vardywatch.com/quiz/) or browse the [quiz archive](https://vardywatch.com/quiz/archive.md).

Each quiz has 5-10 multiple-choice questions with four options, one question at a time and a final score. After answering, a solid green border marks the correct choice and a red border marks a selected wrong choice. Gameplay shows no explanation or repeated answer text; accessible choice labels identify the result. JavaScript is required to play. There is no timer, account or leaderboard. Completed attempts and best/latest scores are stored only in this browser, separated by quiz revision; the archive offers a clear-history control. Storage is not cross-device sync.

Questions use verified completed Premier League fixtures in the selected date range. Manual quizzes use inclusive UTC dates and exclude unfinished matches; scheduled quizzes retain their five-day London-time window. The exact coverage dates and timezone appear at the bottom of each published quiz. Credited goals include penalties and exclude own goals. In new match-scorer questions, wrong choices are verified scorers for that same club in its previous 20 completed Premier League matches, excluding the question's match and allowing cross-season history. The correct scorer is always included. Insufficient history or alternatives omits the question. Other player comparisons use scorers from the selected range; score alternatives are actual recorded scorelines within that window, not predictions. A non-scorer is not proof of non-appearance.

Code establishes facts and answer options; AI selects questions and approved wording. An editor reviews the exact revision before publication. Unsupported questions are omitted; fewer than five supported questions prevents publication. New quizzes include at most two goal-timing statement questions. Opening-scorer and gameweek claims require verified source evidence and are not inferred from unordered goals or calendar dates. Corrections require a newly reviewed revision. An editor can remove an entire published edition; other editions and scores already stored in players' browsers remain untouched.
