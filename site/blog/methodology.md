# How scoring streaks are counted

A run counts consecutive completed Premier League club fixtures in which a player scores at least one credited goal. Penalties count; own goals do not.

A club fixture without a credited goal for that player ends the run, even if the player did not appear. The exports do not provide appearance or lineup information. Runs reset between seasons and on club changes.

Jamie Vardy's benchmark is 11 games in 2015/16. Eleven matches equals that mark; twelve beats it. Future fixtures are opportunities, not predicted goals: later milestones depend on scoring in every intervening club fixture.

Weekly previews cover Thursday 09:00 through Monday end in Europe/London. Players need an active run of at least two club fixtures and a fixture in that window. No previews are scheduled in June or July.

Articles are AI-assisted and reviewed by a human editor. Their source-data cutoff is the oldest upstream fetch timestamp of the required current-season monthly exports, not the time the article or statistics were generated. Publication requires verified source data no more than twelve hours old.
